End of Life

A book whose reader is the caregiver

PythonFlaskOpenAIOllama

Scroll

Breaking bad news, listening to a silence, holding a hand: none of that is learned in a lecture hall. End of Life is an interactive book where a medical student lives through a palliative-care story, makes a choice at every scene, and discovers what those choices say about their relational skills.

The project already existed as a written scenario. My job: turn it into a tool usable by a whole cohort, enriched by AI, and measurable by trainers.

My role

Full design and development: architecture, backend, interface, LLM integration, scoring model, deployment.

01

The scenario as a graph

Each chapter is a graph of scenes, interactions and answers. I built a graphical editor (vis-network) where trainers see the main thread, the branches and the dead ends, and fix the scenario live with automatic link validation. The same graph becomes the player's path mode, their route drawn in green as they go.

  • Scenario editor with live validation
  • Atomic saves with fingerprint, concurrent overwrites refused
  • Mermaid / JSON export of the books
02

AI that writes with the experts, not instead of them

An enrichment module proposes new answers oriented towards a given relational skill (empathy, listening, clarity…), filters duplicates and leaves the decision to the trainer. Over 1,100 choices were generated this way, then reviewed and validated by domain experts. The LLM backend is interchangeable: OpenAI in the cloud, or Llama 3.1 through Ollama locally when the data is sensitive.

  • Strategy pattern OpenAI ↔ Ollama
  • Context-aware prompts (scene, history, target skill)
  • Semantic filtering of redundant proposals
03

Grading soft skills without caricaturing them

The browser only sends the indices of the chosen answers; the server replays the path and computes a score out of 100 per skill, weighted by the relative quality of the options available at each moment. A scoring key was validated against an expert panel: inter-rater agreement ICC = 0.90. At the end of the book, personalised feedback is generated by LLM (with a non-AI fallback).

  • 7 relational skills scored
  • Graded runs vs. practice runs (anti-memorisation)
  • Trainer dashboard, XLSX / CSV / JSON exports, per-participant history
04

Making it production-grade

Clean Architecture (domain, repositories, services, views), admin-created accounts and roles, CSRF, pytest, deployment on Render with Supabase PostgreSQL. The pilot with students is prepared with a protocol and consent documents.

What came out of it

  • 0.90inter-expert agreement (ICC) on the scoring key
  • 1,100+AI-generated choices validated by experts
  • 7relational skills measured
  • 2LLM backends, cloud or 100% local
