April 2026 — todayPersonal project for the 10th edition of a Christian conference on the theme of “Choices”. Published on the App Store and Google Play.

IAssou3

The AI that asks the questions

React NativeExpoSupabaseOpenAI

Scroll

Usually you ask the chatbot a question. Here it's the other way round. Every day the AI comes to the participant: a question on the theme, a challenge to take up, a thought to pursue — and every message ends with “But it's your choice.”

Two hundred people, four days, a single weekend for everything to hold together. The app had to be beautiful, reliable, and never go down during a plenary session.

My role

Everything: product, design, mobile development, Supabase backend, prompts, store publishing, monitoring.

01

An inverted chatbot

The model produces structured JSON outputs: message type, text, options, scoring criteria. Challenges are evaluated automatically and feed a leaderboard. GPT-4.1-mini is the main engine, with a fallback to Llama 3.3 70B via Groq if the main API slows down.

  • Structured outputs validated in Edge Functions
  • Automatic fallback between two providers
  • Automatic challenge scoring
02

The organiser space

From the app, organisers edit the programme, send targeted push notifications, publish alerts and manage content. Everything propagates in real time through Supabase Realtime subscriptions.

  • Live programme
  • Push notifications (Expo Push Service)
  • Row-level roles and permissions (RLS)
03

Shipping for real

Google and Apple sign-in, privacy policy, EAS builds for iOS and Android, over-the-air updates, Sentry to catch errors before users do. The app ran for the whole conference.

  • EAS CI/CD
  • Sentry monitoring
  • 19 versioned SQL migrations

What came out of it

  • ~200users during the conference
  • 2stores: App Store and Google Play
  • 2LLM providers with automatic fallback
