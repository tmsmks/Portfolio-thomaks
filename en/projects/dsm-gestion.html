DSM Gestion

Dictate the quote, the AI lays it out, the manager approves

Next.jsPostgreSQLClaudeOllama

Scroll

A construction business owner spends his evenings retyping quotes. He knows exactly what he wants to invoice — he says it out loud in his van — but between the dictation and the PDF there's Word, a calculator and an hour lost.

DSM Gestion listens. You dictate, type, send an email screenshot or a photo of a scribbled quote: the assistant prepares the document in the company's usual layout. Then the manager reviews, corrects and approves. Never the other way round.

01

AI proposes, a human decides

The assistant runs locally with Ollama (free, no data leaves the computer) or with Claude through the Anthropic API, with structured output validated by a Zod schema. It never invents a price: a line without an amount is flagged, with a suggestion from history if needed. It lists the points to check and shows “before → after” changes. Totals are computed by code, never by the model.

  • Inputs: text, voice dictation, screenshots, photos, PDFs
  • Swappable AI provider: local Ollama or Claude
  • Proposal → review → apply, all logged
02

Compliance as a database

Continuous numbering in the company's format, assigned inside a transaction. An issued invoice is frozen: the app and PostgreSQL triggers refuse any modification; corrections go through credit notes. Every PDF is kept with its SHA-256 fingerprint. Mandatory mentions — ten-year liability, reduced VAT, waste (AGEC law), withdrawal, mediator, penalties — are added automatically depending on the client.

  • Amounts in cents, totals computed server-side
  • Locking by SQL triggers
  • Full audit log: who, what, when, by hand or by AI
03

From quote to payment, in one place

Quote → invoice in one click with deposits deducted, payments received and automatic “paid” status, credit notes, dashboard (to approve, pending, outstanding), Gmail sending with the PDF after confirmation. Installable as an app on desktop and phone, with one account per person: manager, back office, helper.

  • Vector PDFs modelled on the original invoices (@react-pdf/renderer)
  • PWA, local mode with embedded PostgreSQL
  • Vitest tests on a reset database

What came out of it

  • 0prices invented by the AI, by construction
  • 100%of documents approved by a human
  • 1place for quotes, invoices, credit notes, payments and sending
