EcoTracker

A nature library for biodiversity

MongoDBAPI RESTPython

Scroll

A nature library: a library of the living, where everyone adds what they observe. EcoTracker collects species observations, cleans them, stores them and shows them on a map of natural areas.

My role

Data and backend: observation collection via APIs, MongoDB modelling, cleaning, mapping; contribution to the specification and project management (Jira, Notion, Gantt).
