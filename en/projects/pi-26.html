Hospital power outages

Predict the outage before it switches off the OR

LightGBMSHAPPandasStreamlit

Scroll

In a Ugandan hospital, a power cut is not an inconvenience: it's a ventilator stopping. Lacor Hospital has logged its consumption every fifteen minutes for years; nobody had yet tried to read the moment of the next outage in that data.

The goal: estimate the probability of an outage within one hour, three hours, six hours — and explain why.

My role

Data pipeline, feature engineering, model training and comparison, interpretability, Streamlit app.

01

Data that doesn't speak the same language

Lacor's consumption (Uganda), hourly profiles of ten NHS hospitals (UK) and five New York buildings (LL84), Open-Meteo historical weather, EAGLE-I county-level outages, grid load and mix via Electricity Maps. Everything is resampled to the hour and merged into a multi-site dataset.

  • Eight ingestion scripts, one per source
  • Resampling and temporal alignment
  • Target: is_outage, heavily imbalanced
02

Not lying to yourself about validation

With time series, a random split gives beautiful, wrong scores. I enforced TimeSeriesSplit in cross-validation, a chronological hold-out and a rolling-origin walk-forward backtest. Random Forest, XGBoost and LightGBM compared under those conditions: LightGBM wins, with an F1 of 0.763 under strict temporal validation.

  • Temporal, weather and grid-load features
  • GridSearchCV + TimeSeriesSplit
  • Adaptive calibration (isotonic / sigmoid / none) by Brier score
03

Explain, then simulate

SHAP values show what drives the risk up: the load of the last few hours, temperature, time of day. A Streamlit app lets you play with those variables, enter GPS coordinates to fetch the grid zone, and read the risk for the 1h, 3h and 6h horizons.

  • SHAP TreeExplainer, global and local importance
  • One model per forecast horizon
  • Streamlit simulation interface

What came out of it

  • 0.763LightGBM F1 under strict temporal validation
  • 3forecast horizons: 1h, 3h, 6h
  • 16hospital sites across three countries
  • ≈ 0.96ROC AUC in walk-forward backtest
