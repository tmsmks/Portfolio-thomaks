The ney

A reed flute, seven holes, no reed mouthpiece. The sound comes only from the breath and the angle of the lips: it takes months before a clean note comes out. I play the Egyptian ney, the one of taqasim and classical Arabic music — in concert and at events, weddings included — and I make my own instruments from Arundo donax cane I source from Egypt and Turkey.

Deep down it's the same gesture as coding: a lot of patience, a lot of failed attempts, and one day something that rings true. The waves you see in the header of this site are that — a breath becoming a signal.
